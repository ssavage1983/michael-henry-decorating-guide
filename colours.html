Supplier Note

Ordering Colour-Matched Paint

For the main walls, use Dulux Lichen Green 70YY 55/299 as the reference. Choose the curtain colour before finalising the aubergine wall if the curtain match is important.

The hex colours are visual references. The paint supplier colour code/name should be used where possible.

Recommended route: Lichen Green main walls, aubergine music-zone wall, aubergine/plum curtains and natural grey fabric seating.
