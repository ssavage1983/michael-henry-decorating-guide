Preparation

Repairs, vinyl silk preparation and safe wall treatment before the Lichen Green and aubergine paint goes on.

Key Wall Issues

Bright Yellow Vinyl Silk

The existing shiny vinyl silk needs to be de-glossed before painting. Matt emulsion should not be applied directly onto shiny silk because adhesion can be poor.

Bubbled Woodchip

The small bubbled area should be treated as a localised repair. Do not strip the whole room just because of one damaged patch.

Repair Method

Woodchip Bubble Repair

  • Check the area is dry and stable before doing any repair.
  • Use proper wallpaper seam adhesive or wallpaper repair adhesive where the paper can be re-bonded.
  • Inject or brush adhesive behind the bubble, then press flat with a clean sponge or seam roller.
  • Wipe away excess adhesive immediately.
  • If it will not sit flat, cut away only the loose failed section with a sharp craft knife.
  • Fill small damaged areas with Toupret-style interior filler.
  • Stipple the wet filler lightly with a damp sponge to mimic woodchip texture.
  • Prime the repair before painting.

Do Not Use PVA As First Choice

PVA might stick a small wallpaper area down, but it can seal the surface, create shiny patches, affect paint adhesion and make later repairs harder. Use wallpaper seam adhesive first.

Vinyl Silk Prep

Step 1: De-gloss

Lightly sand the shiny yellow vinyl silk with medium-fine sandpaper or a sanding block. The goal is to dull the shine, not rip the wall covering.

Step 2: Clean

Wash with sugar soap, then rinse with clean water. Avoid soaking wallpapered areas.

Step 3: Fill

Fill old picture holes, let them dry fully, sand smooth and remove dust.

Step 4: Prime

Apply Zinsser Bulls Eye 1-2-3 or similar bonding primer before the matt paint coats.

Preparation first, paint second. Good prep is what stops the new finish failing.
