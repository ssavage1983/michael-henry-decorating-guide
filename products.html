Updated Paint Quantity Estimate

Quantity Update

The aubergine is now for the back wall/back alcove music-zone area, not just the original small alcoves. Because the exact final painted area should be confirmed from the room, this guide now allows 5 litres of aubergine/brown-aubergine as a safer practical estimate.
