Room Observations & Constraints

Bright Yellow Vinyl Silk

Issue: Vinyl silk is shiny and slightly flexible. Standard matt emulsion will slide off, cure poorly, or easily scratch off if applied directly.

Action required: The walls must be lightly sanded to remove the gloss, cleaned gently, and then primed with a bonding primer before any topcoats are applied.

Bubbled Woodchip (Approx 1 m²)

Issue: Bubbling on the bottom/front wall area on the right-hand side near the radiator/external wall. The bubbling appears likely to be linked to the cold external wall/radiator-side area, possibly from condensation or temperature difference. Check the area is dry and stable before sealing or painting.

Action required: Treat it as a small localised repair. Do not strip the whole room or plaster.

Bespoke Preparation & Repair Methods

Wallpaper Repair FAQ: Would PVA glue work for this small bubbled area?

Answer: PVA might physically stick small loose wallpaper down, but it should not be recommended as the first-choice repair here.

PVA can cause problems because it may:

  • seal the surface and create shiny patches
  • affect future paint adhesion
  • make later wallpaper repair or removal harder
  • show through paint as a visible patch
  • fail again if the bubbling is linked to cold-wall condensation or moisture movement

1. Repairing the Bubbled Woodchip

  • Re-bond: Use proper wallpaper seam adhesive or wallpaper repair adhesive.
  • Apply: Use a small brush, nozzle, or syringe to get adhesive behind the bubble if possible.
  • Press: Gently press the paper flat with a clean sponge or seam roller. Wipe away excess immediately.
  • Alternative: If the bubbled woodchip will not sit flat, carefully cut away only the failed loose section. Seal the exposed edges. Fill small damaged areas with Toupret-style interior filler. Lightly stipple the wet filler with a damp sponge to mimic the woodchip texture.
  • Prime: Let it dry fully before priming the repaired patch, then painting.

Note: Because the bubbled area is only around 1 m², this should be treated as a small localised repair, not a reason to strip the whole room, plaster the wall, or change the full decorating plan.

2. Preparing the Vinyl Silk Walls

  • Lightly sand the entire wall surface with medium-fine sandpaper to dull the shiny silk finish.
  • Wash the walls gently with sugar soap to remove sanding dust. Do not heavily soak any remaining wallpaper areas.
  • Apply one full coat of a high-adhesion bonding primer (such as Zinsser Bulls Eye 1-2-3).
  • Apply two coats of your chosen trade vinyl matt emulsion.

Step-by-Step Decorating Plan

1

Clear and Protect

Move furniture to the centre. Lay dust sheets. Mask skirting, the 3.81m entry doorway frame, radiator, and light switches.

2

De-gloss the Vinyl Silk

Lightly sand the bright yellow walls to break the shiny surface. Wash down with sugar soap. Allow to dry.

3

Localised Wallpaper Repair & Fill Holes

Inject or brush wallpaper repair adhesive behind the 1 m² bubbled area near the radiator. Press flat. Fill old picture holes flush. (Allow overnight dry).

4

Prime All Walls

Sand the picture-hole filler smooth. Spot-prime the wallpaper repair. Apply a full coat of Zinsser Bulls Eye 1-2-3 primer over all yellow walls. (Allow 1-2 hrs dry).

5

Paint Main Walls (Pale Green)

Cut in and roll the Pale Green main walls (West, North, South bay). Do not overload the roller. (Allow 2-4 hrs dry).

6

Paint Alcoves (Aubergine)

Mask the chimney breast return edge. Cut in and roll the 1.29m North alcove and 1.20m South alcove with Aubergine. (Allow 2-4 hrs dry).

7

Second Coats & Clean Up

Apply second coats to both colours for an opaque finish. Remove tape carefully while the paint is still slightly damp.

Big Tip: Safety & Masking

  • Ventilate: Always ventilate the room well when sanding and painting.
  • PPE: Wear eye protection and a suitable mask when sanding the vinyl silk and the dried filler.
  • Radiator Access: Protect the floor beneath the radiator thoroughly, and use a mini-roller on a long handle to reach behind it without removing it.

Final Finish Checklist

  • Bubbled woodchip safely re-bonded or filled.
  • Picture holes filled and sanded smooth.
  • Vinyl silk walls entirely coated in bonding primer.
  • Dust removed from all walls and skirting.
  • Edges masked securely (chimney/alcove split).
  • Two full topcoats applied.
  • Tape removed carefully while slightly damp.
  • Leftover Aubergine and Pale Green paint labelled.
