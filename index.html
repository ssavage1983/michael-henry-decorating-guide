Decorating Guide

Room: Ground floor front Victorian living room
Prepared for: Michael Henry

Multi-page guide for the Lichen Green living room, aubergine music zone, furnishings, costs and decorating plan.

Overview

Main Scheme

  • Main living walls in Lichen Green: #A8B595 / Dulux 70YY 55/299.
  • Aubergine or brown-aubergine used for the back wall/back alcove music zone.
  • Warm wood, antique brass, cream textiles and muted plum/rust accents.
  • No property address is shown in the guide.

New Layout Idea

  • Back wall/alcove becomes the music and production zone.
  • Low shelving separates the music area from the living/relaxing zone.
  • Guitars can become a wall feature if safely fixed and not overcrowded.
  • Mirror above the blocked fireplace helps bounce light around the room.
