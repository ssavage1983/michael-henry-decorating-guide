Checklist / PDF

Printable decorating checklist, updated for Lichen Green main walls and aubergine music-zone wall.

Equipment Checklist

Preparation

  • Dust sheets or polythene roll
  • Masking tape, ideally 2 rolls
  • Sugar soap and sponge
  • Craft knife and syringe/nozzle if available
  • Wallpaper seam repair adhesive
  • Toupret interior filler
  • Sandpaper or sanding block
  • Clean cloths and PPE

Painting

  • 9 inch roller frame
  • 3 medium-pile roller sleeves
  • Angled cutting-in brush
  • Paintbrush set
  • Paint tray
  • Paint kettle
  • Zinsser 1-2-3 primer
  • Lichen Green and aubergine paints

Finishing

  • Small artist’s brush for touch-ups
  • Decorators caulk if gaps need filling
  • Bin bags
  • Spare tape
  • Cleaning cloths
  • Labelled paint pots

Step-by-Step Decorating Plan

1

Plan Zones Before Painting

Confirm exactly where the aubergine music-zone wall begins and ends. Mark the green/aubergine split lightly with low-tack tape.

2

Clear and Protect

Move furniture to the centre, protect floors, mask skirting, sockets, doorway edges and radiator areas.

3

De-gloss the Vinyl Silk

Lightly sand the shiny yellow vinyl silk to dull the surface. Clean with sugar soap, rinse and allow to dry.

4

Repair Woodchip and Fill Holes

Repair the bubbled woodchip locally, fill old picture holes, let filler dry, sand smooth and remove dust.

5

Prime Surfaces

Spot-prime repairs, then apply a full coat of bonding primer over the vinyl silk walls.

6

Paint Main Walls Lichen Green

Cut in and roll the main living-room walls in Lichen Green. Do not overload the roller on textured woodchip.

7

Paint Music Zone Aubergine

Cut in and roll the back wall/back alcove music-zone area in aubergine or brown-aubergine. Take time on the colour split line.

8

Apply Second Coats

Once dry, apply second coats of both colours for a solid finish that hides the old yellow.

9

Plan Drilling After Paint Cures

Wait until paint has cured properly before fitting shelves, guitar hangers or mirror fixings. Check wall type, cables and safe fixing points first.

Final Finish Checklist

Before Painting

  • Bubbled woodchip repaired or trimmed back.
  • Picture holes filled and sanded smooth.
  • Vinyl silk fully de-glossed.
  • Walls cleaned and dry.
  • Bonding primer applied.

After Painting

  • Lichen Green walls have full coverage.
  • Aubergine music-zone wall has clean edges.
  • Tape removed carefully while paint is slightly damp.
  • Touch-up paint labelled and stored.
  • Wall fixings planned only after paint has cured.
