Personal Music Story

Make The Music Zone Feel Like Michael

This room should not feel like a generic home studio. Michael has a strong music and theatre background, including music production work, theatre-related music, and time with The Flying Pickets and The Grasshoppers. The aubergine back wall/music zone should feel creative, personal and performance-led.

  • Use the aubergine or brown-aubergine wall as a stage-like backdrop for the computer, electric piano and production setup.
  • Add shelves for music books, theatre notes, small framed memorabilia, headphones and production accessories.
  • Display the 3 guitars as part of the room’s story, not just as storage.
  • Keep the clarinet safely stored or displayed away from heat, moisture and direct sunlight.
  • Consider one small framed section for Flying Pickets, Grasshoppers or theatre/music memories.
  • Use warm lamp light, brass/aged gold frames and dark wood to make the music area feel cosy and theatrical.

Correct Seating Plan

2-Seat Sofa Bed + 1 Single Armchair

The seating should be one compact grey 2-seat sofa bed plus one separate single armchair. Do not use a normal 2-seat sofa if guest sleeping flexibility matters, and do not replace the single armchair with a second sofa because it will make the room feel crowded.

2-Seat Grey Sofa Bed

Best direction: compact grey sofa bed with storage, so the living zone works for sitting every day and occasional sleeping without adding bulky furniture.

  • Look for hidden storage if possible.
  • Keep the width compact so it does not block the music-zone route.
  • Grey fabric is safest with Lichen Green walls and aubergine curtains.

1 Single Grey Armchair

Best direction: one comfortable single armchair in a similar grey fabric. It gives another seat without making the front room feel over-furnished.

  • Choose slim arms if floor space is tight.
  • Use a small side table or floor lamp beside it.
  • Second-hand is fine if the fabric is clean and neutral grey.

Foldable / Modular Table With Storage

Best Table Type

Best direction: a drop-leaf or folding table with built-in shelves/drawers and stools that tuck underneath. Wheels help it move between dining, project work and music/admin use.

Why: it works hard without permanently eating the middle of the room.

  • Choose storage drawers/shelves before choosing a larger tabletop.
  • Make sure stools tuck fully under or inside the unit.
  • Keep it folded day-to-day unless it is actively being used.

Liked Amazon / Mondeer Option

The liked option suits the room because it is rustic brown/black, folds down, has stools, drawers, shelves and wheels. It should sit against a side wall or the edge of the living zone, not permanently in the centre.

Fit warning: check the full open size before buying. If it blocks the walking route to the sofa bed or music zone, use it as an occasional fold-out table only.

Aubergine Curtains First

Match Curtains Before Final Paint

Curtain colours are limited, so choose the closest aubergine/plum curtains first, then adjust the final feature-wall shade if needed. This is safer than painting a perfect aubergine and then discovering no curtain fabric matches it.

Argos aubergine curtains Dunelm aubergine curtains Dunelm plum curtains Amazon aubergine curtains

Dyeing Curtains To Match

If ready-made aubergine curtains are too far off, buy white or light natural-fibre curtains and dye them. Dylon is best for cotton, linen or viscose; it will not dye pure polyester properly, and polycotton usually dyes lighter.

  • Check the curtain fabric label before buying dye.
  • Weigh the dry curtains so the dye quantity can be calculated.
  • Use machine dye for larger washable curtains where the care label allows.
  • Test with a small fabric sample first if possible.

Cheap, Free & Upcycled Sourcing

Free or Nearly Free Local Finds

Check local free and second-hand sources before buying new. Search daily and sort by newest because good sofa beds, armchairs, shelves and side tables go quickly.

Upcycling / Refinishing

The word is probably upcycling or refinishing. Look for cheap solid-wood side tables, low shelving, bookcases or TV units, then sand, clean and finish them in warm wood stain, black-brown, aubergine or antique brass details.

  • Use second-hand low shelving as the divider between music and living zones.
  • Paint mismatched units the same colour to make them look intentional.
  • Replace cheap handles with aged brass or blackened bronze handles.
  • Use baskets or fabric boxes to hide music cables and accessories.

Low-Cost Shelf & Alcove Ideas

Scaffold-Board Style Shelves

For the alcoves, use reclaimed scaffold boards or budget shelf boards with black or antique-brass brackets. This suits the Victorian/music-room feel and gives strong visual weight against the aubergine wall.

B&Q scaffold boards Wickes scaffold boards IKEA shelf boards B&Q shelf brackets

Music & Memorabilia Shelves

Use shelves for music books, headphones, production notes, theatre memories and small framed Flying Pickets or Grasshoppers references. Keep heavier gear on lower units, not high wall shelves.

  • Use black or antique-brass brackets for a more intentional look.
  • Keep cable boxes and adapters in baskets or labelled boxes.
  • Leave breathing space around display pieces so it does not feel cluttered.

Woodchip Texture & Light Effect

Why The Colour Will Shift

Woodchip wallpaper catches light on the raised flecks and creates tiny shadows in the dips. Lichen Green will look softer and slightly mottled; aubergine will look deeper in the shadows and warmer where light catches the texture.

Instruments, Mirror & Comics

3 Guitars

Use the guitars as a wall feature, but keep spacing generous. A staggered three-guitar arrangement can look better than a tight row.

Clarinet

Keep the clarinet safe, dry and away from radiator heat or direct sun. A closed case on a shelf is the safest option.

Mirror & Comics

Use an aged gold, antique brass or warm wood mirror above the blocked fireplace, and keep Marvel comics in UV-protective sleeves or acrylic display.
